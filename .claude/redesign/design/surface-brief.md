# Kite app window

Scope: the whole browser window (sidebar, page area, command bar, menus, sheets, Settings,
start page, first run, little window). Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: any Mac user, most from Safari or Chrome, using Kite all day to reach
pages and keep the important ones within reach. First-time sidebar users. Task comes first;
the page is the content, the browser is the tool around it.

Constraints: macOS conventions for controls, type and layout; light or dark follows the
system; name and kite icon stay; text 4.5:1, accent 3:1; keyboard actions never animate.

Memorable moment: the magenta string drawing across the top of the page while it loads.

Unresolved: translucent (vibrancy) sidebar on macOS; signed builds and auto-update.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A browser drawn on a chart of still air. It refuses both category defaults: the
grey window with one blue accent, and the pastel gradient sidebar.

OWN-WORLD: Chart-paper shell tinted sea-glass by day and night-chart slate after dark; a
white or deep-slate page card on a hairline; fine contour lines of a calm pressure field,
never moving; one chart magenta used only for focus, the primary action, switches, links
and the string. San Francisco at 13px regular, 11px chart labels, tabular numerals.

STORY: The visitor sees a quiet, exact Mac app, understands the sidebar holds their pages,
trusts that nothing moves unless work is happening, and makes it their default browser.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sidebar left (address field, Favorites, Pinned, Today, Space dots). Page
card right. On a new tab: greeting, one 44px search field centered on the contour field,
Favorites below. The string runs along the card's top inner edge while loading.

FORM: an aeronautical and weather chart; candidate 3 of 7 on the ordered list;
seed:294b5aa866c76a2b.
